About Rajan Path Lab

Rajan Path Lab is a leading diagnostic and pathology center dedicated to delivering accurate, reliable, and high-quality laboratory testing services. Established with a vision to make advanced diagnostics accessible to everyone, we are committed to supporting better healthcare through precision, innovation, and patient-centered care.

Our laboratory is equipped with modern diagnostic technology and operated by experienced professionals who follow stringent quality control procedures to ensure dependable and clinically relevant results. From routine health screenings to specialized investigations, every test is performed with the highest standards of accuracy and professionalism.

At Rajan Path Lab, we understand that timely diagnosis plays a vital role in effective treatment. Therefore, we focus on fast sample processing, prompt report delivery, and complete transparency throughout the testing process. Our goal is not only to provide laboratory reports but also to contribute to better health outcomes by helping doctors and patients make informed medical decisions.

Over the years, we have earned the trust of patients, physicians, and healthcare professionals through our commitment to excellence, ethical practices, and exceptional service quality. We continuously strive to improve our facilities, expand our diagnostic capabilities, and maintain the highest standards of laboratory medicine.
